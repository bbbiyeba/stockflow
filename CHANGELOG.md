# Changelog

## v1.0.1 (2026-10-18)

Fixes the forecast bias found in the final review (AUDIT.md D13 and D14).

**What changed**
- **Bias calibration:** each segment's LightGBM forecast (ABC class × demand pattern) is multiplied by actual ÷ forecast from earlier backtest months only.
  - Thin segments are shrunk toward 1 with 1,000 pseudo-units, and factors are capped at ±25%.
  - The held-out month is still scored with factors it never saw.
  - The final forecast uses factors from all four months, written to `reports/bias_calibration.csv`.
  - Turn it off with `forecast.bias_calibration: false`.
- **Cause:** checking a different training sample design ruled out sampling, and the simple models were not biased. At 10× the global model fits ~490k series less tightly, and an under-fit log-link model errs low.

**Effect on the base network (25 branches, 300 SKUs)**
- **Bias:** held-out bias −0.8% → 0.0%; A-class lumpy series −7.2% → −0.2%. Daily WAPE unchanged at 49.8%; 28-day error 12.8%.
- **Knock-on:** slightly higher forecasts put 419 positions at risk (was 411). Two runs of the new code give identical results. The plan is now 895 transfers saving a net $216k, $11k more than greedy, and avoiding 8,948 expected lost-sale units. Fill rate rises from 93.5% to 99.0%.
- **Refreshed:** the README, dashboard screenshots, demo snapshot, model card, FAQ, demo script and slide deck were all updated to these numbers. The slide 7 screenshot crops are now generated by `presentation/make_charts.py`.

**Effect at 10×**
- **Bias:** held-out bias −2.6% → −0.8%. Branches and categories over ±5% fall from 9 to 2.
- **The 2 that remain** (B031 −6.5%, B205 −5.4%) were on target in the earlier months, and branch bias doesn't carry over between months. A per-branch calibration stage was tried and removed, because it changed them by under a point and added noise at 1x.
- **Accuracy:** daily WAPE is 51.1% (was 50.8%), against 75.8% for seasonal naive.
- **Optimiser:** 160 parallel batches (159 optimal, 1 at its time limit with the best plan kept) save $44.8M against $42.7M for greedy, with 0 sanity-check violations.
- **Resources:** the forecast stage takes 3,984 s with a peak of 10.2 GB on a 4-core, 15 GB machine.

## v1.0.0 (2026-10-13)

First complete release of Stockflow: branch × SKU demand forecasting and inventory rebalancing, demonstrated on a synthetic 25-branch, 300-SKU network.

**Forecasting**
- **Models:** global LightGBM plus five simple models, chosen per segment on three backtest months and reported on a fourth.
- **Accuracy:** held-out daily WAPE is 49.8%, against 74.9% for seasonal naive (34% better). Bias is −0.8%, and 84% of actuals fall inside the P10–P90 range.
- **Where it loses:** reported both by group and series by series.

**Inventory policy and rebalancing**
- **Policy:** safety stock, reorder points, target levels, stock-out risk, and excess / stagnant / liquidation flags.
- **Optimiser:** a MILP (PuLP + HiGHS) with arc pruning, parallel SKU batches and a greedy fallback.
- **Result:** 883 transfers save a net $203k ($10k more than greedy). They free $1.1M of excess, raise the expected fill rate from 93.9% to 99.0%, and raise turns from 8.4 to 8.9.
- **Robustness:** sensitivity re-solves keep 66–92% of the plan, and savings stay between $171k and $235k.

**Dashboard**
- **Pages:** transfer recommendations with approve / reject and an audit log; inventory health; forecast explorer with plain-language drivers; forecast accuracy.
- **Transparency:** every impact figure has its formula and assumptions.
- **Security:** optional OIDC sign-in, roles and financial masking.
- **Hosted demo:** a view-only copy is deployed on Vercel.

**Engineering**
- **One command:** `python run_pipeline.py` regenerates everything, and runs are deterministic.
- **Checks:** 64 tests at 94% coverage; ruff, black, mypy, bandit and pip-audit are clean.
- **Docker:** the image is built and run end to end in CI.
- **Scale:** the 10× network (250 branches, 3,000 SKUs, about 490k series) runs end to end on a 4-core, 15 GB machine in 2.4 hours, re-verified on the final code. All 163 optimiser batches solve to optimality, saving $42.8M against $40.6M for greedy.

**Documentation:** README, model card, FAQ, 3-minute demo script, data contract, monitoring plan, security notes, audit report and final review. The slide deck (.pptx and .pdf) is built from the pipeline outputs.
